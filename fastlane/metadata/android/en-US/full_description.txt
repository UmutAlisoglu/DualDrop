DualDrop gets photos and videos off your DJI Osmo Action camera and saves them to your phone's gallery. On many phones it also keeps your home Wi-Fi and internet connected while the camera is connected, which the official app can't do.

Features:

* Browse everything on the camera as a grid, sorted by day.
* Select many photos and videos at once: long-press to start, tap more, or tap a date to select the whole day. Download them all with one tap.
* Preview and trim videos straight from the camera before downloading, or save a single frame as a photo.
* Keep home Wi-Fi: a one-time setup inside the app (about two minutes, no computer, no root) lets your phone stay on your home Wi-Fi while it talks to the camera. It switches itself back on after every restart. DualDrop checks whether your phone supports it.
* No account, no login, no activation, no ads.

Tested with the DJI Osmo Action 4 and a Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. The camera part is based on Osmosis by Konrad Iturbe, which also supports Osmo Action 5 Pro, Action 6, Osmo Nano and Osmo Pocket 3 / 4.

Privacy: DualDrop talks only to your camera. No analytics, no accounts, no cloud uploads.

Requirements: Android 10 or newer, Bluetooth LE. The "keep home Wi-Fi" feature needs Android 11 or newer and a phone whose Wi-Fi chip supports two connections.
