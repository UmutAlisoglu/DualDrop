# Changelog

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-07)

- The app now says "DualDrop" instead of "Osmosis" in the Bluetooth and Wi-Fi hints and in the log e-mail subject.
- The "Last action" time on the Keep home Wi-Fi screen now uses your phone's own date and time format.
- New README for everyone: what the app does, how to install it, step-by-step guides, questions and answers, and troubleshooting.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-07)

First public release, based on Osmosis 1.5.0.

- Keep your home Wi-Fi while connected to the camera: one-time setup in the app via Wireless debugging (no computer, no root), switched back on automatically after every restart.
- Easier multi-select and downloads in the gallery: long-press to start selecting, tap a date to select the whole day, "Open gallery" after downloading.
- New name, icon and design.
